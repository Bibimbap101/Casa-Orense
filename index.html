About the House

Crafted in the Philippines, for the world.

Casa Orense is a Philippine-based high fashion brand inspired by the timeless elegance of European fashion. Built on the idea of sophistication, craftsmanship, and modern luxury, Casa Orense blends classic aesthetics with contemporary Filipino creativity.

Each piece is conceived at our Manila atelier and realized by the hands of the country's finest artisans — a declaration that Filipino luxury needs no apology, no footnote.
